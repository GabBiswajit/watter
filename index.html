Server Rules

To ensure everyone has a great experience on Watter Craft, please follow these rules:

Be Respectful

Treat all players with respect. No harassment, bullying, or toxic behavior will be tolerated.

No Cheating

No hacking, exploiting, or using unauthorized mods. Play fair and enjoy the game as intended.

Clean Chat

Keep the chat family-friendly. No spam, excessive caps, or inappropriate language.

No Griefing

Don't destroy or steal from other players' builds. Respect everyone's hard work.

Listen to Staff

Follow instructions from moderators and admins. They're here to help maintain a fun environment.

Report Issues

If you encounter problems or rule violations, report them to staff or use our Discord server.

Note: Breaking these rules may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on the severity. Let's keep Watter Craft a fun place for everyone!
